DATE: September 28, 2025

GOD'S WILLINGNESS TO HELP MANKIND

🧠📝 Memory Verse

For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost. - Luke 19:10

Text: Matthew 8:1-3 📚

INTRODUCTION 🧠✨📖

The occurrences on planet earth and man's approaches to curbing them has not been very successful because man can't help himself. God made man to depend upon Him and that is why he is willing to offer the very assistance necessary at any time of need. This gift of Eve to Adam is a demonstration of God's willingness, having seen Adam's inability to cope. You need God to make it and He is willing to help you.

AIMS AND OBJECTIVES 🎯📖💡

AIMS: To address the wrong conclusion of men been master-minded by Satan that God is partial and unwiling to help even though he sees them in problem. ⚠️🕊️📖💰🤔

OBJECTIVES: That man may receive God's help through the right approach.⚖️📖💰🤔

Text Review/Lesson 📚✨

Lesson 📖💡

A great multitudes had gathered unto Jesus in Matthew 5 and he went up to a mountain to educate them without demand. This was an offering to save the lost and dying people. Hosea 4:6 says the people are destroyed for lack of knowledge and knowledge fills the house with goodies - Prov. 24:4.

  1. Following With Right Purpose: 🎯👣

    You are among the many people who profess and follow Christ, but what is your purpose?
    Except your purpose is right, although he is willing, you won't get help.

  2. Understanding The Time and State:

    While everybody was busy following, the purposeful leper took advantage of the moment. The Lord had been with God and ministering to men, and he thought, “The presence of the Lord is here; no time could be better.” Most people do not know when God is moving, and that is why they remain unhelped.

  3. God is Not Partial 🌀🔥

    Lepers are rejected by men but when he came to Jesus, he was treated not as an outcast. God is not partial, come now and received help from Him. John 6:37, Acts 10:34.

  4. Motivate God For Help 🌀🔥

    Whatever you need from God is possible but it will be easier received through motivation. Solomon motivated God and was blessed - 2 Chron. 1:6-7. The leper worshipped God while others where just following and received attention and help.

  5. Call on God in Faith 🌀🔥

    God regards faith - Hebrews 11:6, Matt. 8:10,13. The leper's request made in faith gave him help. Walk with God in faith.

Conclusion 📜

God is the God of all flesh and is willing to help anyone irrespective of person. Apply the right approach and you will get the right answer.
